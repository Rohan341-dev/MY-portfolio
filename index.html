01 / About

A little about me.

"I started with curiosity.
Now I build with purpose."

I didn't start with a plan. I started with a browser, a broken HTML page, and the question: "How does this actually work?"

That question led me down a path I never fully mapped out — from writing my first lines of HTML and CSS, to building full-stack applications, exploring AI and large language models, launching SaaS products, and experimenting with systems that solve real problems.

I'm Rohan Yadav, publicly known as Rohan Codekage. I'm a student programmer and full-stack developer based in Nepal. I'm still young, still learning, and still very much at the beginning — but I've already built things I'm genuinely proud of.

My journey has taken me from basic frontend work into backend systems, databases, APIs, full-stack development, SaaS architecture, AI/LLM experimentation, automation, and product development. I've built management systems, e-commerce platforms, AI tools, and experimental hardware projects.

My philosophy is simple:

"Don't just learn technology. Build something with it."

I enjoy the entire process — taking an idea from Idea → Research → Design → Code → Debug → Improve → Ship. Every project teaches me something new, and every failure becomes the foundation for the next attempt.

My interests extend beyond traditional software development. I'm curious about AI and how language models can be integrated into real products. I experiment with computer vision, automation, SaaS architecture, EdTech, e-commerce, hardware like Arduino, and physics-inspired systems. I'm drawn to problems that sit at the intersection of technology and the real world.

I don't just want to learn technology. I want to build with it. And I want to build things that matter.

2024 — Present PRODUCT SAAS

SajiloCode

A technology & product initiative focused on software, education, and real-world business systems.

Role Founder / Developer
Focus SaaS · EdTech · Business Systems · E-commerce
Problem

Schools, restaurants, and small businesses in Nepal often rely on manual processes, paper-based records, or expensive imported software that doesn't fit their specific needs. There's a gap for affordable, localized, and practical digital solutions.

Solution

SajiloCode was created as a product lab — a space to build and ship real-world systems. Starting with a School Management System and expanding into Restaurant Management, CRM, Billing, SaaS platforms, and business automation tools. Each product is designed to solve a specific problem while learning how to think like a product builder, not just a coder.

School Management

Student records, attendance, scheduling, results, and administration.

CRM

Customer relationship management for business operations.

Restaurant System

Order management, billing, inventory, and operations.

SaaS Platform

Multi-tenant software products and business automation.

Stack: Python Django HTML CSS JavaScript SQLite PostgreSQL REST APIs
What this represents

Moving from coding into product thinking and entrepreneurship. Building systems that solve real problems while learning how to create sustainable technology ventures.

2024 — 2025 SYSTEM COMPUTER VISION GPS

Smart School Route Management System

A school transportation and safety platform — GPS tracking, route management, driver monitoring, and real-time alerts.

Role Lead Developer
Focus Safety · GPS Tracking · Route Optimization · Computer Vision
Problem

School transportation systems often lack visibility. Parents don't know where the bus is. Schools can't monitor routes in real time. Safety incidents go undetected. There's no centralized system for managing school fleet operations.

Solution

A comprehensive system combining GPS tracking, route management, driver monitoring, real-time dashboards, timestamps, safety alerts, and camera-based monitoring using computer vision concepts. The platform provides schools and parents with real-time visibility into transportation operations.
